Oopbuy shopping guide

A better shortlist in three steps.

For Oopbuy finds, use a repeatable research process. Keep facts from the listing separate from your preferences and any details you still need to confirm.

01.

Collect the original information

Save the source link, item name, category, selected variant and displayed price. Include the currency and the date you checked the listing.

02.

Compare fit and construction

Check measurements, fabric descriptions, closure details and available close-up photos. Mark missing information as unknown instead of guessing.

03.

Review the complete cost

Separate the product price from delivery and other applicable charges. Reopen the listing before paying to check the current variant and terms.

Oopbuy product spreadsheet

Make every row useful.

A product comparison sheet works best when entries follow the same format. These fields help you compare like with like without relying on a product title alone.

Suggested fields for your own shopping spreadsheet
Field What to record Why it helps
Source & date Original product URL, seller identifier and date checked. Lets you revisit the exact listing and identify information that may be outdated.
Variant & category Color, size, model reference and the relevant fashion category. Reduces confusion between similar photos and different product options.
Size & measurements Listing size chart, units and relevant garment or footwear measurements. Makes fit comparisons more useful than a size label by itself.
Materials & photos Disclosed composition, detail images and any unanswered questions. Keeps documented information separate from visual assumptions.
Cost & currency Item price, delivery estimates, fees and applicable tax or duty estimates. Supports a more complete budget comparison.
Status & decision Needs checking, shortlisted, rejected or purchased, with a brief reason. Keeps your research organized as your preferences change.

Oopbuy size guide

Measure before you choose.

Use an item you already own as a reference. Compare the listing's measurements using the same units and measurement method wherever possible.

Fit notes worth saving

  • Shoes: the sizing system, stated length measurements and any fit guidance.
  • Tops: chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length and total garment length.
  • Bottoms: waist, rise, inseam and leg opening where available.
  • Headwear and jewelry: circumference, internal dimensions and adjustability.

Budget & photo checks

Look beyond the listed price.

An inexpensive item can have a different total cost after delivery and applicable charges. Keep estimates clearly labeled until they are confirmed.

Estimated total = items + domestic delivery + service fees + international delivery + payment fees + applicable taxes or duties.

Some charges may already be included or may not apply. Check the actual order breakdown to avoid counting the same fee twice.

For Oopbuy QC photos, if available through your chosen service, compare the photographed item with the selected variant and requested measurements. Photos can reveal visible details, but they do not establish authenticity or guarantee performance.

Direct answers

Before you open another product link.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-link organizer used to collect and compare shopping finds. Useful entries include the original listing, category, size information, listed price, photo references and the date checked. It does not guarantee product quality, availability or authenticity.

Does this page provide an official Oopbuy spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent editorial guide and category directory. It does not provide an official Oopbuy spreadsheet, live inventory or a downloadable product database. Shopping links lead to CAFANS.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the listing's measurements with an item that already fits you. For shoes, check the stated sizing system and available length measurements. For clothing, compare chest, shoulder, sleeve, waist and garment length where relevant.

What costs belong in a shopping spreadsheet?

Record the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international delivery, payment or currency-conversion fees and any applicable taxes or duties. Keep estimates separate from confirmed charges.

Do brand images confirm authenticity or availability?

No. The images are visual references and are not verified CAFANS inventory. A brand name, photograph or spreadsheet entry does not establish authenticity, seller authorization or current availability.
